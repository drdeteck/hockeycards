Tips for a clean read
  • Shoot straight on. Small tilts are corrected automatically, but strong angles blur the stripes.
  • Get close. The fine horizontal lines in the scrambled box must be visible when you zoom in, so crop or move closer rather than shooting the whole table.
  • Avoid glare. Toploaders and slabs reflect light; tilt the card slightly away from the lamp or take it out of the sleeve.
  • Wrong box detected? Drag a rectangle over the scrambled panel on the left image to decode exactly that area.
  • Low confidence? Try a sharper photo, or toggle B&W and judge the decoded text by eye.
  • SAMPLE promo cards have a thin scrambled strip with the message on one line instead of the regular two-line panel. The decoder reads both and labels the strip as SAMPLE.
  • Sharing: add ?img= plus an image address to this page's link to open it pre-loaded.

How the decoding works

  1. Find the panel. The scrambled box is the only area covered in fine horizontal lines, so the decoder looks for that texture and checks that the lines repeat evenly.
  2. Straighten and measure. It corrects any tilt, then measures the line spacing and where each white mask line falls across the panel.
  3. Remove the mask. The white lines are dropped and the gaps filled in from the rows between them, which reveals the hidden text. This is the image on the right.
  4. Name the message. The revealed text is compared with the two known messages, SORRY / TRY AGAIN and SPECIAL / ARTIST'S PROOF, on one line for SAMPLE strips and two lines otherwise. Confidence shows how clearly one beats the other. It is a match, not character-by-character reading, so trust the decoded image if they ever disagree.
